Right-hand traffic, single-lane ring, cars circulate anticlockwise and give way to traffic already on the ring. Each car's speed is limited by the bend it's on (sideways grip ≈ 0.22 g), so tight ends of a stretched roundabout or a sharp turn-in from a skewed road slow cars down, and slower entries need bigger gaps.
